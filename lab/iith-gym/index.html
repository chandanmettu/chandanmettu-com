      How to use this

      1. Look around. Drag to turn the model, scroll or pinch to zoom. Walk in puts you inside at eye height.
      2. Switch layouts. Pick a layout at the bottom. The equipment moves there so you can watch the change happen.
      3. Rearrange. Press Rearrange to start your own proposal, then drag any machine. Turn it with the round handle or the R key.
      4. Check spacing. A red zone means a machine is too close to a wall or another machine, by published gym spacing guidance.

      Keys: 1 Walk in, 2 Overview, 3 Floor plan, 4 Tour. In Walk: W A S D or the arrows. Esc closes things.

      The hall and its machines are rebuilt from photos. Sizes and positions are estimates until the hall is measured.
